Rann Utsav 2026–27: Opening, Tent City & How to Reach Kutch

Every winter, the monsoon drains off the Great Rann and leaves a salt floor that glares white at noon and turns silver under a full moon. Rann Utsav 2026–27 opens on 1 November 2026 at Dhordo and runs until 7 March 2027.

This guide covers the dates, the Tent City and the part most guides skip: getting from Bhuj to the salt without losing half a day.

Check Bhuj to Dhordo Cab

Rann Utsav 2026–27 Dates and Opening

Rann Utsav - The Tent City is run by Evoke Experiences with Gujarat Tourism. The 2026–27 season stretches from 1 November 2026 to 7 March 2027, close to four months.

The first fortnight is the quietest. Crowds build through December and peak around full moon weekends and New Year.

Some third-party sites list 5 November to 4 March. The official website lists 1 November to 7 March, so treat that as the reference and recheck before paying for non-refundable tickets.

Full Moon Nights and the Best Time to Visit the White Rann

The salt looks whitest from late December through February. Full moon nights are the headline act, and the windows listed for this season are:

  • 23 to 25 November 2026
  • 22 to 24 December 2026
  • 21 to 23 January 2027
  • 19 to 21 February 2027

Uttarayan on 14 January 2027 brings kite flying to Bhuj and Dhordo. Desert nights can fall to single digits, so proper layers matter more than a stylish jacket.

Full moon and holiday dates sell out first, for tents and for cars alike.

Inside Rann Utsav Tent City: Stays, Food and Experiences

Stays range from Swiss cottages and premium tents to Rajwadi and Darbari suites. Meals are vegetarian, with Kutchi dishes and Jain food available.

Evenings belong to folk music and dance, including Siddi community performances and instruments like the jodiya pava, santar and dholak. Days bring craft stalls, wellness sessions, adventure activities and stargazing under very little light pollution. Some activities are chargeable, so check what the chosen package covers.

Evoke has also added 35 seasonal tents near its Dholavira property this season, for travellers exploring Dholavira and Khadir Bet.

The White Rann sits close to the international border, so entry needs a permit. Tent City guests have it arranged through their booking. Day visitors apply on rannpermit.gujarat.gov.in and should carry photo ID.

How to Reach Rann of Kutch: Distance and Travel Time to Dhordo

Dhordo is about 85 km from Bhuj, the nearest airport and railhead. Bhuj Airport (BHJ) connects with Ahmedabad, Mumbai and Delhi, some routes seasonal. Trains link Bhuj with Ahmedabad, Mumbai, Delhi and Surat, and GSRTC and private buses run to Bhuj as well.

One-Way Taxi or Round-Trip Cab: Choosing the Right Kutch Plan

If you're arriving in Bhuj by air, or if you're beginning and ending your trip in various locations, then one way taxi is a clever choice as you're only paying for the miles you actually travel. If you're planning on road-tripping from Ahmedabad, Rajkot or Vadodara, or headed to Kala Dungar, Bhuj, Mandvi or Dholavira, then a round trip cab is the best bet: one driver, one vehicle, the entire trip. There's only one fare negotiation to deal with.

Prices vary by distance you will travel, type of cab (Hatchback, Sedan, SUV, Tempo traveller), number of days required and time of your journey. Beyond the Tents: Kutch Places Worth an Extra Day

If you have an extra day,

  • Check out Kala Dungar, the highest point in Kutch for great salt flat views.
  • Then there's the 'Road to Heaven' and Dholavira, a UNESCO World Heritage Harappan city.
  • Bhuj has the Aina Mahal, Prag Mahal, and the craft village Bhujodi.
  • Mandvi is a beach town where they build ships.

A multi-day round-trip cab can easily cover these parts of your trip around Tent City.

Quick Travel Tips for Rann Utsav 2026–27

  • Book the Tent City, permit and taxi together for full moon and holiday dates.
  • Keep a buffer of two to three hours between landing at Bhuj and any fixed check-in plan.
  • Carry photo ID for every traveller, plus layers, sunscreen and a power bank.
  • Confirm exact dates and special dates on the official Rann Utsav website before travel.
