Frequently Asked Questions

What is a one-way cab service?

A one-way cab lets you travel from one city to another without paying for the return journey.

Is there a return fare?

No, you need not pay the return fare. We charge only a one-way price for a one-way drop-off taxi. For the best price for your one-way drop-off taxi on your travel date, please enter trip details and check.

Can I get a cab at midnight?

Yes, we offer 24x7 one-way cab service.

Are toll charges included?

Toll charges may be included or billed separately, depending on the route.

Can I book a one-way cab for outstation travel?

Yes, you can book a one-way cab for outstation travel also.

Can we pick up additional passengers on the way in one way cab?

One way cab includes only one pickup and one drop-off. Additional pickup or drop-off will incur additional charges.

Do I need to pay both sides toll tax for a one-way cab?

For a one-way trip, you need to pay the one side toll tax only.
