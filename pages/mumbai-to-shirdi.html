Primary Spiritual Site

Temple Complex & Activities
Dwarkamai: Mosque where Sai Baba spent considerable time, sacred dhuni (fire) still burning
Chavadi: Where Sai Baba slept alternate nights, Thursday processions recreate this tradition
Gurusthan: Neem tree where young Sai Baba was first seen meditating
Lendi Baug: Garden Sai Baba maintained, a peaceful meditation spot
Dixit Wada Museum: Sai Baba's life, artefacts, photographs, history
Aartis & Rituals
Four Daily Aartis: Kakad (4 AM), Madhyan (12 PM), Dhoop (evening), Shej (night)
Thursday Special: Palki procession from Samadhi Mandir to Chavadi (evening)
Prasad: Receive blessed prasad after darshan
Accommodation & Facilities
Shri Saibaba Sansthan Trust: Provides accommodation (book in advance)
Private Hotels: Numerous options, all price ranges, near the temple
Dining: Many restaurants offering vegetarian food, prasad meals
Shopping: Religious items, Sai Baba photos/idols, books
Nearby Temples (Optional Extensions)
Shani Shingnapur (70 km): Unique Shani temple, village famous for houses without doors (trust in Shani Dev)
